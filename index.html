Pochta

Pochta is a personal email client for Android that shows your Gmail mailbox in an iPhone-style interface.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

What the app accesses. When you choose "Sign in with Google", Pochta requests the scope https://mail.google.com/ so it can read, send, move and delete messages in your Gmail mailbox over IMAP and SMTP. Access is used only to display your mail and perform the actions you request in the app.

Where data is stored. Messages and account settings are stored only on your device. Access tokens are kept in Android encrypted storage. Pochta has no servers: your data is never sent to the developer or to any third party, and it is not used for advertising, analytics or AI model training.

Sharing. Data is not sold, shared or transferred to anyone. The app connects only to Google servers (imap.gmail.com, smtp.gmail.com and Google sign-in).

Google API Services. Pochta's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access. Tap "Выйти из аккаунта" in the app, or revoke access at myaccount.google.com/connections. Uninstalling the app deletes all locally stored data.

Contact. Questions: open an issue at github.com/dvdelta-beep/pochta-privacy/issues.

Политика конфиденциальности (кратко)

Почта — личный почтовый клиент для Android. Доступ к Gmail используется только для показа писем и действий, которые вы выполняете в приложении. Всё хранится только на телефоне (токены — в зашифрованном хранилище), у приложения нет серверов, данные никому не передаются. Отозвать доступ: кнопка «Выйти из аккаунта» или myaccount.google.com/connections.
